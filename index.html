The problem

Two problems. One dead card.

Zombie subscriptions

You pay for what you forgot

The gym you don't visit, the app you stopped opening. Other tools nag you to cancel — they still make you do the deciding.

Agents with real cards

One bad prompt drains an account

As AI agents start paying for things, a stored card with no controls is a liability. A card that dies after one charge caps the blast radius to exactly one.

How it works

A dead card turns every renewal into a decision.

The card is the enforcement layer. The memory is the intelligence layer. Together, a subscription has to justify itself.

1

Each subscription gets its own card

The merchant charges it once. Then the card closes. Nothing can silently auto-renew.

2

The renewal bounces off a closed card

When the merchant tries to bill again, the charge declines — and that decline triggers a decision.

3

Vouch reads your real usage

It pulls your history — plays, rides, logins — scores the renewal against price changes and overlap, and decides.

4

Renew, hold, or ask — with the reason

Worth it? A fresh card is minted and the charge goes through. Not worth it? Vouch asks you out loud, and you cancel in one word.

Plain-English decisions

It always shows its work.

Renewed

Spotify. 47 plays in 30 days, price unchanged. New card minted and charged.

Held

Peloton. 0 rides since July 3. Price up 15%. You also pay for ClassPass.

Asked

NYT. 3 articles in 30 days and the price just jumped 25% to $17. Worth keeping?

What's inside

Built to say no when it should.

Single-use cards

Every subscription sits on its own card that closes after one charge. The blast radius is exactly one card.

Usage-aware memory

Vouch remembers how you actually use each service, so the decision reflects real behavior — not a default.

Voice at the moment

When a call needs you, Vouch asks out loud with the number. Answer "renew" or "cancel" in a word.

Budget you can see

Category caps and a live spend chart show what you commit — and what Vouch kept in your pocket.

Live transaction feed

Every charge, decline, and refund streams in as your issuer authorizes it, tagged and explained.

You override anything

Every decision is yours to reverse, and the reason is always on screen. Vouch earns auto-renew over time.
